Privacy Policy for Speech

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Overview

Speech is designed to read local PDF documents aloud using on-device speech synthesis. The app does not require an account and does not provide a cloud service.

Data Collection

Speech does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

PDF files selected by the user are processed locally on the device. Extracted text remains on the device and is used only to provide reading, navigation, highlighting, and speech playback functionality.

Third Parties

Speech does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or external speech services.

Speech and Accessibility Settings

Speech uses the voices and speech-related settings already configured by the user in the operating system. No voice recordings are created by the app.

Background Audio

Background audio is used only to allow speech playback to continue while the app is not in the foreground.

User Control

Users choose which PDF files to open. Removing a PDF from the device, or closing it in the app, stops Speech from using that document.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information provided on the App Store listing for Speech.
